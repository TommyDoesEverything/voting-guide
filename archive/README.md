# Archive: the original prototype

`gemini-prototype.html` is the first version of this guide, generated by an AI chat (Gemini). It's kept as a record of where the project started. **It isn't a source and shouldn't be relied on.**

## What was wrong with it

Fact-checking against official sources found:

- **Mislabelled polling.** Its "Leger poll" chart used Metro Vancouver-wide figures from Leger's October 2026 poll as if they were Burnaby's. The current guide shows the Burnaby-specific results with their small sample size ([Leger's report](https://leger360.com/metro-vancouver-municipal-election-2026-poll/)).
- **Wrong or invented candidate details.** It had a duplicate "Allen Chen (Ind)" and platforms swapped between candidates (for example, Hsu's and ABCROBINHOOD's). It also described Martin Kendell as a provincial candidate, which Elections BC's candidate list doesn't support.
- **Missing key facts.** David Xie has said he's no longer seeking his council seat but is still on the ballot, and two municipal candidates are also on the October 24 provincial ballot.
- **Loaded language.** It used phrases like "boondoggle", "fiscal illusion", "right-wing populist" and "mathematically unviable", plus unsourced "strategic insights" predicting outcomes.
- **Opinion presented as fact.** Its unattributed "pros", "cons" and party labels such as "Main Right-Wing Opposition" were written in the guide's own voice.

## What the current guide does instead

- Candidate statements come from the City of Burnaby's official profiles, reproduced verbatim.
- Party positions come from each party's own platform.
- Arguments for and against are labelled as opinion, and each one names who said it.
- Every added fact links to a source.
